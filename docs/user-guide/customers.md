# Customers

**Menu:** Customers · **Address:** `/customers`  
**Who:** Owner, manager, and clerk (clerks need this for Counter and credit sales).

---

## Why this screen exists

A **customer** is a party you **sell to**. The Counter and Sales screens both **require** a customer. There is no “no bill name” sale.

Naming the shop on **Start** already creates **Walk-in**. Create named parties for anyone who takes credit. If you used full Business setup instead of Start, create Walk-in once here.

**Open** a customer to see **what they owe** and every invoice.

You can also create a customer from the left of **Sales**. Use this page for the full list, archive, and outstanding view.

---

## Create / edit (left)

Heading: **Create customer** or **Edit customer**.

| Field | Required | What to type |
|-------|----------|----------------|
| **Customer name** | Yes | Max 150 characters. Example: `Apex Builders` or `Walk-in`. |
| **Contact person** | No | Max 120 characters. Example: `Neha Shah`. |
| **Mobile number** | No | 7–20 phone characters if filled. Example: `+91 9998887776`. |
| **Address** | No | Max 255 characters. Example: `Ring Road`. Useful if you deliver. |

Click **Create customer** or **Save customer**.

**New customer** clears the form after you opened someone.

---

## List (right)

- **Search customers** — name, contact, or mobile
- **Show archived**
- Cards: name, contact, mobile, address, **Active** / **Archived**

| Button | What it does |
|--------|----------------|
| **Open** | Loads the form and shows outstanding + sale history |
| **Archive** | Hides from default lists and pickers |
| **Restore** | Brings them back |

Empty: **Create your first customer to track sales and dues.**

---

## After you Open a customer

### Outstanding balance

- **Invoices** — count
- **Billed** — net billed (after returns)
- **Paid**
- **Due**

Collect money on **Sales** (the page links there).

### Sale history

All sales for this customer, including **Cancelled**.

Each card: sale number, date, status or Cancelled, total, returned (if any), net, paid, due.

To print or take payment, go to **Sales**, search the sale number, **View details**.

---

## When to choose what

| Situation | What to do |
|-----------|------------|
| Unknown cash buyer at Counter | Select `Walk-in` (create it once here) |
| Builder who pays at month end | Real name + mobile; leave **Amount paid now** as 0 on the sale |
| Wrong duplicate party | Archive the duplicate; keep the one that already has invoices |
| Customer moved away, still owes | Do **not** archive until dues are settled, or you will hunt with Show archived |

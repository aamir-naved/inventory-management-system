# Everyday jobs

Use this page when you know **what you want to do** but not **which screen**. Each job is a short recipe. Full field-by-field help is on the linked screen pages.

If you have never signed in, start with [First day](first-day.md) instead.

---

## Switch the app to Hindi

After sign-in: left menu (phone: **Menu**) → bottom **Language** → **हिन्दी**.

This covers the sidebar, top bar, and **Counter**. Sales, Products, Reports, and the rest stay in English. The choice is remembered on this browser.

## Open the shop in the morning

1. Open the app in the browser and **Sign in** (mobile OTP or email).
2. Land on **Counter** and bill. Read **Dashboard** when you want today’s totals, low stock, and dues.
3. Click **Alerts** in the top bar.
4. If a lorry is due, an owner/manager uses **Purchases**.

On a phone, tap **Menu** first. To use Hindi on the menu and Counter, set **Language** at the bottom of the sidebar. More: [Around the workspace](workspace.md).

---

## Sell cash with a barcode

1. **Counter**
2. **Customer** is already `Walk-in` after you named the shop
3. Click **Barcode**, scan (or type `CEM-001` and press **Enter** if you are using the sample cement)
4. Leave **Amount paid** blank if they paid the full total
5. **Complete sale**, then **Share bill** (or **Print**)

If Counter shows **You are offline**, reconnect first. Full screen: [Counter](counter.md).

---

## Sell on credit

1. **Customers** — create the party if they are new (name + mobile helps later).
2. **Sales** — select them, **Add item**, enter quantities and prices.
3. Set **Amount paid now** to `0`, or type an advance.
4. **Record sale**. **Print invoice** if they want a copy.

Collect later: [Collect money a customer still owes](#collect-money-a-customer-still-owes). Full screen: [Sales](sales.md).

---

## Sell several items by name (no barcode)

Use **Sales**, not Counter. Click **Add item**, search **Product** by name or SKU, type **Quantity** (for 15 bags, type `15`).

---

## Give a special price on one invoice

**Sales** → add the product → change **Price per unit** on that line only. The catalog selling price stays the same for the next bill.

Counter always uses the catalog selling price.

---

## Print or reprint an invoice

**Sales** → search the sale number or customer → **View details** → **Share bill**, **Print invoice**, or **Download invoice**.

From **Counter**, use **Share bill** / **Print** on the sale you just saved.

Supplier bills: **Purchases** → **View details** → **Print bill** / **Download bill**.

---

## Collect money a customer still owes

1. Open **Sales** (or **Customers** → **Open** the party to see their invoices).
2. **View details** on the invoice that still has **Due**.
3. **Amount** is filled with the remaining due. Lower it for a part payment.
4. **Notes**: `UPI`, `Cash`, cheque number, and so on.
5. **Record payment**.

The chip becomes **PARTIAL** or **PAID**.

---

## See who owes us

- Fast: Dashboard card **Outstanding customers**, or **Alerts** for invoices older than 7 days.
- Full list: **Reports** → **Customer dues**.
- One party: **Customers** → **Open**.

---

## Customer returned goods

1. **Sales** → find the original invoice → **View details**.
2. Under **Record sale return**, type the quantity that came back (not the whole bill unless they returned everything).
3. Fill **Reason** (example: `Wrong grade`).
4. **Record sale return**.

Stock increases. Do **not** cancel the whole sale unless the entire invoice was a mistake and nothing has been returned yet.

---

## Wrong sale — void the whole invoice

Owner or manager only, and only if **no returns** exist on that invoice.

**Sales** → **View details** → type **Cancellation reason** (required, example: `Billed twice`) → **Cancel sale**.

Stock is restored. Money that was already recorded shows as a **Refund** in payment history. Clerks must ask an owner or manager.

If some quantity was already returned, cancel is blocked. Return the rest instead.

## Add a new customer

**Customers**, or the left-hand form on **Sales**:

- **Customer name** is required
- Contact person, mobile, address, and **State code** (for GST IGST) are optional

Click **Create customer**. Full screen: [Customers](customers.md).

---

## Add a walk-in / cash customer

Create **one** customer named `Walk-in` only if you used full Business setup instead of **Start**. Quick-start already creates Walk-in. Reuse it on Counter for unknown buyers.

Create a named customer when you need their phone, delivery address, or credit history.

---

## Buy stock from a supplier

1. **Suppliers** — create the company if new (or use the left form on **Purchases**).
2. **Purchases** — select the supplier, set **Purchase date**, **Add item**, quantity and **purchase** price (what you pay, not what you sell at).
3. **Amount paid now** — what you paid with this bill (`0` if the supplier gave credit).
4. If GST is on and the supplier is in another state, set their **State code** first (there is no Interstate tick on the bill).
5. **Record purchase**.

Stock goes up. The product **cost price** becomes a weighted average of old stock and this bill. Full screen: [Purchases](purchases.md).

---

## Pay a supplier

**Purchases** → **View details** on the unpaid bill → **Record payment**.

---

## Send goods back to a supplier

**Purchases** → **View details** → **Record purchase return** → quantities you are sending back → **Record purchase return**.

Stock goes down. You cannot cancel a bill that already has returns.

---

## Wrong purchase bill

If you have **not** recorded returns: type **Cancellation reason** → **Cancel purchase**.

If you already returned some lines: record another return for what is left. Cancel stays blocked.

---

## See what we owe suppliers

Dashboard **Outstanding suppliers**, **Reports** → **Supplier dues**, or **Suppliers** → **Open** a party.

---

## Add products one by one

**Products** → name, unit, cost price, selling price, opening stock, barcode / HSN / GST % if you use them → **Create product**.

Click **New product** for the next item. Full screen: [Products](products.md).

---

## Add many products from Excel

1. **Products** → **Download Excel** (this gives you the correct headers).
2. Fill rows. **Name** and **Unit** are required. Save as `.xlsx`.
3. **Upload Excel**.

If some rows fail, fix the lines listed under **Rows that need a fix** and upload again.

---

## Make Counter scanning work

1. **Products** → **Edit** the item.
2. Put the barcode in **Barcode** exactly as the scanner types it (scan once into that box, or watch Counter while you test).
3. Each barcode can belong to only one product.
4. On Counter, click the **Barcode** field so it is focused, then scan.

If you do not use barcodes, bill from **Sales** and search by name.

---

## Check how much stock I have

- One item: **Inventory**, search the name.
- Shop value: Dashboard **Inventory value**, or the cards on **Inventory**.
- File for paper/accountant: **Reports** → **Inventory** → **Download Excel**.

---

## Fix stock after a physical count

Example: the screen shows 100, you counted 102.

**Inventory** → **Adjust stock** on that product → **Adjustment quantity** `2` → **Reason** `Physical count` → **Apply adjustment**.

If you counted 97, type `-3`.

Do not create a fake purchase just to move the number.

---

## Record damage or missing stock

**Inventory** → select the product → **Adjustment quantity** negative (example `-2`) → reason `Damaged` or `Shortage` → **Apply adjustment**.

---

## See low stock / overdue alerts

Top bar: **Alerts (N)**.

- Low stock → **Inventory** (tick **Low stock only**) then **Purchases** to restock.
- Customer payment overdue (sale date more than 7 days ago) → **Sales** → **Record payment**.
- Supplier payment overdue → **Purchases**.

Set each product’s **Low stock threshold** on **Products**. New products can start from the default in **Settings**.

---

## Stop stocking an item without deleting history

**Products** → **Archive**. Old invoices still show the name.

To sell it again: tick **Show archived** → **Restore**.

The same Archive / Restore pattern exists on **Customers** and **Suppliers**.

---

## Turn on GST / put GSTIN on invoices

**Business** (or **Settings**):

1. Tick **Enable GST on invoices and bills** (Settings: **Enable GST on invoices**).
2. Fill **GSTIN**, **State code**, **State**.
3. **Save**.
4. On each product, set **HSN** and **GST %**.
5. On **Business**, upload a **logo** if you want it on the PDF.

New invoices then show tax. Old bills are not rewritten.

---

## Charge IGST (other state)

There is no Interstate tick on the bill.

1. Shop state is **State code** on **Business**.
2. On the **customer** (sales) or **supplier** (purchases), fill **State code** with the other state’s two digits (example `27`).
3. Counter / Sales / Purchases then show **Interstate (IGST)**.

Leave the party state **blank** (or the same as the shop) for local tax (CGST + SGST). Walk-in cash sales with no state stay local.

---

## Prices already include GST

On Business or Settings, tick **Prices include GST** / **Selling and purchase prices include GST**.

Tick this when the rupee you type is the shelf rate **including** tax.

Leave it **off** when you type a net rate and want the app to add 5% / 12% / 18% / 28% on top.

## Give Excel to the accountant

**Reports**:

| They asked for | Tab | Also set |
|----------------|-----|----------|
| Sales for a month | Sales | From date and To date |
| Purchases for a month | Purchases | Same dates |
| GST working | GST | Same dates. Read **Output tax**, **Input tax**, **Net tax** |
| Who hasn’t paid | Customer dues | — |
| What we owe | Supplier dues | — |
| Godown stock | Inventory | Optional: Low stock only |

Then **Download Excel**. This does **not** file GSTR on the government site.

Product master: **Products** → **Download Excel**.

## See today’s business

**Dashboard**. “Today” follows the shop time zone (default `Asia/Kolkata`).

## Invite a counter person

Owner only: **Team** → their **Email** → role **Clerk — sales counter** → **Send invite**.

They open the email within **7 days**, enter name and password, and land on Counter.

They must **not** click **Create account** on the public login page (that would be a separate owner login). Use the invite link.

Full screen: [Team](team.md). Join steps: [Sign-in and account](sign-in-and-account.md).

---

## Invite someone who can buy and report

Same as above, but choose **Manager — shop operations**.

Managers can run Products, Purchases, Reports, Business, and Settings. They cannot invite or remove staff.

---

## Stop a staff login

Owner: **Team** → **Remove access** on that person.

If they have not joined yet: **Revoke** on the pending invite.

---

## Change a clerk into a manager

There is no “edit role” button.

1. **Remove access** on the clerk.
2. **Send invite** to the same email as **Manager**.
3. They open the new link and join with **the same password** they already use.

## Change my name or password

**Profile** → change **Full name**, or fill **Current password** and **New password** (at least 8 characters) → **Save profile**.

You will need to sign in again on this device and any other device that used the old password.

Email cannot be changed here.

---

## I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen: **Forgot password?** → your email → open the mail → **New password** → **Reset password** → sign in.

---

## Change shop name or logo

**Business** → edit name, address, GST → **Save changes**.

Logo: after the business exists, choose a PNG or JPEG file of **512 KB or less**. **Remove logo** if you uploaded the wrong file.

---

## Something is missing from the menu

You are signed in as a **clerk**. Purchases, Suppliers, Reports, Activity, Business, Settings, and Team invites are for the owner (and most of those also for a manager).

If that person should buy stock and run reports, the owner should [change them to a manager](#change-a-clerk-into-a-manager).

---

## The app will not let me sell (stock)

The sale would take quantity below zero, and **Allow negative stock** is off.

Do one of these:

- Record the **purchase** (or raise **opening stock** if this is day-one stock never entered)
- **Adjust stock** if the count in the shop is really higher
- Owner/manager: turn on negative stock in **Settings** only if you bill first and enter the purchase later

---

## I cannot find a product, customer, or bill

- Clear the search box, or search a shorter piece of the name.
- Tick **Show archived** for products, customers, and suppliers.
- Sales and purchases search by **number**, party name, or **notes**.
- Long lists use **Previous** / **Next** (25 per page).
- Counter’s customer dropdown only lists up to 100 people — use **Sales** search if the party is missing there.

---

## No email arrived

Verification, password reset, and invites need mail.

- Click **Resend email** on the yellow banner (verification).
- Ask the person who installed the shop to check SMTP, or on a local Docker install to copy the link from backend logs (`Mail not configured; logging outbound message`).
- Invites expire after **7 days** — send a new one if the link is old.

---

## Print did not open

The sale is still saved. **Sales** → that sale → **Print invoice**.

Allow pop-ups for this site if the browser blocked the print window.

---

## I typed the wrong amount paid

You cannot edit a payment line after save.

- If you **under-paid**: **Record payment** for the remaining due.
- If you **over-stated** cash on a brand-new mistaken invoice: owner/manager **Cancel sale** (no returns) and create it again.
- If the invoice is otherwise correct, settle the extra outside the app (refund in UPI/cash) and leave a **note** on the sale.

There is no separate “refund voucher” screen. Cancelling a paid invoice writes a **Refund** line in payment history.

---

## Counter says I am offline

The phone or PC cannot reach the server. **Complete sale** is disabled.

Reconnect Wi‑Fi or mobile data. Do not assume a bill saved. Check **Sales** if you already tapped Complete sale during a blip.

---

## The screen crashed

You see **This screen crashed** with **Reload page** and **Go home**.

Saved work is on the server. Reload, then check **Sales** / **Purchases** before entering the same bill again.
